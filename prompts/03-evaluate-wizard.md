# Prompt 03: Evaluate Wizard

> Use for beginner-friendly dependency and impact analysis. Do not require the user to know ENCORE or BRF.

## Plain Explanation

- Dependency = what the business needs from nature.
- Impact = what the business changes, consumes, pollutes, or disturbs in nature.

## ENCORE / IBAT Guidance

- Use ENCORE as the primary beginner Evaluate screen for activity-level dependencies, pressures, and materiality ratings.
- If the user does not know the ISIC code, infer candidate ISIC/activity from the business description, label it as an internal classification assumption, and ask for confirmation.
- Do not treat ENCORE as site-specific biodiversity evidence or as an official TNFD risk score.
- Use IBAT/location evidence only as a modifier: it can raise the priority of pressures/dependencies when a site is near or within a biodiversity-sensitive area, but only if public or licensed evidence is provided.

## Questions

Ask up to 5:

1. Which business activities are most important?
2. Does the business use a lot of water, land, crops, livestock, timber, minerals, fish, or energy?
3. Does it generate wastewater, solid waste, air emissions, land conversion, noise, or habitat disturbance?
4. Which suppliers or raw materials are most important?
5. Any known environmental complaints, permits, penalties, or customer ESG requests?
