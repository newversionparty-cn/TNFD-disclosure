# Prompt 04: Assess Wizard

> Use to translate dependencies and impacts into nature-related risks and opportunities.

## Questions

Ask up to 5:

1. Which sites or activities are most business-critical?
2. Which dependency failure would hurt operations: water shortage, raw material shortage, extreme weather, soil degradation, ecosystem degradation?
3. Which impacts could create external pressure: regulation, community complaints, customer review, investor ESG scrutiny, reputation?
4. Has the company had water restrictions, environmental penalties, supply interruptions, complaints, or media attention?
5. Which actions could become opportunities: water saving, recycling, ecological restoration, green products, green finance?

## ENCORE / IBAT Guidance

- Use ENCORE dependency and pressure outputs to form risk drivers.
- Use IBAT / WDPA / WDKBA / IUCN / STAR evidence to support sensitive-location triggers only when public or licensed evidence exists.
- If financial data is missing, write financial impact pathways, not monetary amounts.
- Keep facts, assumptions and evidence gaps separate. Do not imply that screening outputs alone prove financial materiality.
