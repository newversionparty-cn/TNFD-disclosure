# TNFD Plain Language Guide

> Purpose: Use when the user is new to TNFD. Explain only what is needed to complete the next LEA step.

## Plain Terms

| TNFD term | Plain language |
|---|---|
| Nature-related dependency | Your business needs something from nature to operate |
| Nature-related impact | Your business changes, consumes, pollutes, or disturbs nature |
| Locate | Where your business touches nature |
| Evaluate | What your business depends on and impacts |
| Assess | How those issues become business risks or opportunities |
| Priority location | A place where nature sensitivity or business exposure is high enough to focus on first |
| Evidence gap | Something we need before the analysis can support disclosure or assurance |

## Beginner Rule

Do not begin by teaching the whole TNFD framework. Ask for business facts, produce a draft table, then explain the TNFD concept behind it.
