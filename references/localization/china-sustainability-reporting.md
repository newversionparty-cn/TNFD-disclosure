# China Sustainability Reporting Alignment

> Purpose: Use when the user asks about China-listed companies, A/H shares, exchange reporting, biodiversity disclosure in China, or whether TNFD is mandatory.

## Source Status

- Source type: China sustainability-reporting alignment summary.
- Last reviewed locally: 2026-04-29.

## Core Distinction

China exchange sustainability reporting requirements are not the same as TNFD. TNFD is a voluntary nature-related disclosure framework that can support nature and biodiversity reporting.

## Mandatory Reporting Check

Ask:

1. Is the company within the mandatory sustainability-reporting scope under the exchange rules for the reporting period?
2. Is the company listed both domestically and overseas?
3. Is nature, biodiversity, ecosystem protection, water, pollution, land use, agriculture, forestry, mining, energy, real estate, or finance material to the company?
4. Does the company want to voluntarily align its nature-related disclosure with TNFD?

## Suggested Wording

Use:

> You may be required to publish a sustainability report under Chinese exchange rules. TNFD itself is not mandatory in China, but it can be used as a voluntary framework to structure nature and biodiversity-related disclosures.

Avoid:

> You are required to disclose TNFD.

## Mapping Logic

| China reporting need | TNFD support |
|---|---|
| Biodiversity/ecosystem protection topic | LEAP Locate/Evaluate and TNFD Strategy/RIM disclosures |
| Water resource topic | Location-specific dependency/risk assessment and metrics |
| Pollution and ecological impact | Impact drivers and mitigation response |
| Supply-chain responsibility | Value-chain scoping and RIM-A(ii) |
| Governance and risk management | TNFD Governance and RIM-C |
