# TNFD 14 Recommended Disclosures

> Purpose: Use for drafting, gap analysis, and assurance-readiness checks against TNFD recommended disclosures.

## Source Status

- Source type: Official TNFD recommendations summary.
- Last reviewed locally: 2026-04-29.

## Governance

| ID | Recommended disclosure | Evidence to request |
|---|---|---|
| G-A | Board oversight of nature-related dependencies, impacts, risks and opportunities | Board charter, minutes, committee papers |
| G-B | Management role in assessing and managing nature-related dependencies, impacts, risks and opportunities | Management responsibilities, policies, reporting lines |
| G-C | Human rights policies and engagement activities relating to Indigenous Peoples, Local Communities and affected stakeholders | Human rights policy, engagement plan, grievance records |

## Strategy

| ID | Recommended disclosure | Evidence to request |
|---|---|---|
| S-A | Nature-related dependencies, impacts, risks and opportunities over short, medium and long term | LEAP outputs, risk register, opportunity register |
| S-B | Effects on business model, value chain, strategy and financial planning | Strategy papers, capex plans, value-chain analysis |
| S-C | Resilience of strategy to nature-related risks and opportunities, considering scenarios where appropriate | Scenario analysis, sensitivity analysis, assumptions |
| S-D | Locations of assets and activities in priority locations | Asset coordinates, priority-location methodology |

## Risk And Impact Management

| ID | Recommended disclosure | Evidence to request |
|---|---|---|
| RIM-A(i) | Processes for identifying, assessing and prioritising nature-related dependencies, impacts, risks and opportunities in direct operations | LEAP method, direct operation evidence |
| RIM-A(ii) | Processes for identifying, assessing and prioritising nature-related dependencies, impacts, risks and opportunities in upstream and downstream value chains | Supplier/customer scope, proxy methods, traceability plan |
| RIM-B | Processes for managing nature-related dependencies, impacts, risks and opportunities | Controls, mitigation plans, monitoring |
| RIM-C | Integration into overall risk management | ERM policy, risk taxonomy, governance workflow |

## Metrics And Targets

| ID | Recommended disclosure | Evidence to request |
|---|---|---|
| M-T-A | Metrics used to assess and manage material nature-related risks and opportunities | Risk/opportunity metrics, method notes |
| M-T-B | Metrics used to assess and manage dependencies and impacts on nature | Dependency/impact metrics, core global metrics table |
| M-T-C | Targets and goals used to manage nature-related dependencies, impacts, risks and opportunities and performance against them | Target baseline, target year, owner, performance data |

## Use Rules

- Use the IDs above in report maps and gap analysis.
- Do not compress G-C into a generic "stakeholder" item; it includes human rights and affected stakeholder engagement.
- Do not mark S-D complete without location-specific evidence.
- Do not mark RIM-A(ii) complete if value-chain evidence is absent.
