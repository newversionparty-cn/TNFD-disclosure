# TNFD LEAP Components

> Purpose: Use this file when running scoping or LEAP work. It distinguishes official TNFD LEAP components from internal consulting deliverables.

## Source Status

- Source type: Official TNFD guidance summary.
- Primary sources: TNFD Recommendations v1.0 and LEAP approach guidance v1.1.
- Last reviewed locally: 2026-04-29.

## Core Rule

Do not say "TNFD LEAP has no L1-L4". TNFD LEAP includes official components for Locate, Evaluate, Assess, and Prepare. If the skill uses a consulting maturity scale, name it `M1-M4` or `Q1-Q4`.

## Scoping Components

| Component | Question | Skill output |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | What are the organisation's activities and assets? | Business activity and asset boundary |
| C2 | What are the entry points for LEAP? | Priority business lines, geographies, or value-chain nodes |
| C3 | What type of analysis is feasible? | Evidence plan and data availability rating |
| C4 | What resources and governance are needed? | Workplan, owners, and escalation points |

## Locate Components

| Component | Question | Skill output |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Span of the business model and value chain | Direct operations and value-chain map |
| L2 | Dependency and impact screening | Initial high-dependency/high-impact activities |
| L3 | Interface with nature | Biome, ecosystem, and ecosystem-service interface table |
| L4 | Interface with sensitive locations | Sensitive and priority location register |

## Evaluate Components

| Component | Question | Skill output |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Identification of environmental assets and ecosystem services | Dependencies and environmental assets list |
| E2 | Identification of dependencies and impacts | Dependency and impact pathways |
| E3 | Dependency and impact measurement | Metrics, evidence, and data-quality rating |
| E4 | Impact materiality assessment | Prioritised material dependencies and impacts |

## Assess Components

| Component | Question | Skill output |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Risk and opportunity identification | Risk and opportunity longlist |
| A2 | Adjustment of existing risk mitigation and management | Current controls and control gaps |
| A3 | Risk and opportunity measurement and prioritisation | Heat map, financial pathways, and prioritisation |
| A4 | Risk and opportunity materiality assessment | Material risks/opportunities for disclosure |

## Prepare Components

| Component | Question | Skill output |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Strategy and resource allocation plans | Response strategy and resource implications |
| P2 | Target setting and performance management | Nature-related targets, KPIs, and owners |
| P3 | Reporting | Draft disclosures mapped to 14 recommendations |
| P4 | Presentation | Final disclosure pack, evidence index, and limitations |

## Consulting Deliverable Mapping

Use this mapping as an internal delivery aid, not as official TNFD wording:

| Phase | Common deliverables |
|---|---|
| Scope | Project charter, entity boundary, value-chain boundary, stakeholder plan |
| Locate | Asset/location register, sensitive-location screen, priority-location list |
| Evaluate | Dependency/impact matrix, ENCORE/BRF screening output, evidence gaps |
| Assess | Risk register, opportunity register, financial impact pathways, scenarios |
| Prepare | 14-recommendation map, metrics table, target plan, response roadmap |
